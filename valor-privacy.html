About this policy

Virginia Valor FC is a nonprofit youth travel soccer club based in Chantilly, Virginia, and the travel club of the Chantilly Youth Association (CYA) and Southwestern Youth Association (SYA). This Privacy Policy explains how Virginia Valor FC collects, uses, shares and protects information through virginiavalorfc.org (our "Website") and the registration, event and contact forms linked from it.

By visiting our Website or by permitting your child to use our Website, you are agreeing to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Children under 13

Our Website is intended for parents, guardians, coaches and other adults. Player registration and event sign-ups are completed by a parent or legal guardian, and the information about a player comes from that parent or guardian.

We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not send us any personal information, and ask a parent or guardian to contact us instead. We recommend that parents talk with their children about not sharing personal information online with anyone they don't know.

If we learn that we have collected personal information directly from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it. If you believe we might have such information, please contact us at info@virginiavalorfc.org.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use our Website and programs, we may collect:

  • Parent and guardian contact details, such as name, email address, phone number and mailing address.
  • Player information provided by a parent or guardian at registration, such as name, date of birth, gender, age group, team and emergency contacts.
  • Correspondence, such as questions you send us, our responses and the email addresses involved.
  • Employment-related information from coaching and staff applicants.
  • Order and event details, such as team rosters and apparel sizes for club events.
  • Technical information collected automatically when you visit, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and the site that referred you.

Where it comes from

  • Directly from you, when you fill in a form on our Website, register for a program or event, sign up for club email or contact us.
  • From the service providers that run our registration and team management, such as PlayMetrics.
  • Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies described below.
Cookies and analytics

Our Website is hosted by Wix.com. Wix uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the Website working and secure and to give us statistics about how it is used, such as which pages are visited and how often. We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Website. We use this information only to improve the Website, and we do not combine it with registration information to identify you.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. Some parts of the Website may not work properly without cookies.

Our Website does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no common industry standard for them.

Photos and video

Virginia Valor FC may photograph or film practices, games, camps and club events, and share those images on our Website and social media to celebrate our players and teams. We do not post a player's contact information with a photo.

If you do not want photos or video of your child shared by the club, email info@virginiavalorfc.org. We will stop using new images of your child and remove existing ones from our Website and accounts where we can.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect:

  • To run our programs, including registration, tryouts, team placement, schedules, events and camps.
  • To communicate with families, players, coaches and staff, including club news and announcements.
  • To process fees, orders, donations and financial aid, including billing and collection.
  • To register players and coaches with leagues and governing bodies, as their rules require.
  • To review coaching and staff applications.
  • To keep our Website working, secure and easy to use.
  • In any other way we describe when you provide the information, with your consent, or to comply with any applicable law.
Service providers we use

We rely on outside services to run the club. They receive information only as needed to provide their services to us:

  • Wix.com, which hosts our Website and its contact and interest forms.
  • PlayMetrics, which runs registration, payments, team rosters and team communication.
  • Google, for club email and the forms and spreadsheets used for some events, such as the 3v3 Community Cup, and for Google Analytics.
  • Square, which processes donations and some fundraising payments.

Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy.

When we share information

We may share personal information:

  • With the service providers listed above, to support club operations.
  • With leagues and governing bodies, such as VPSL, ECNL RL, NCSL, USL-Y, VYSA and US Club Soccer, to register players, teams and coaches.
  • To fulfill the purpose for which you provide it.
  • For any other purpose we disclose when you provide the information, or with your consent.
  • To comply with any court order, law or legal process, including to respond to any government or regulatory request.
  • If we believe disclosure is necessary or appropriate to protect the rights, property or safety of Virginia Valor FC, our players, families or others.

We may share aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify anyone.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Payments

Fees, orders and donations are processed by our payment providers, such as PlayMetrics and Square. Virginia Valor FC does not receive or store full card or bank account numbers.

Where your information is held

Information may be held at our offices and by our service providers. Information you provide to us will be transmitted to and stored in information technology facilities and databases maintained in the United States of America and, for some providers, elsewhere. By submitting information to us, you consent to the transmission and storage of your information in those locations.

How long it is kept

We keep personal information while a player, family or staff member is active with the club. After that, we keep it only as long as necessary:

  • To respond to any questions, complaints or claims made by you or on your behalf;
  • To show that we treated you fairly; or
  • To keep records required by law or by our leagues and governing bodies.

We do not keep children's personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected. Different retention periods apply for different types of information.

Your choices and rights
  • Review, correct or delete. A parent or guardian can ask to review the personal information we hold about their child, have it corrected or deleted, or stop further collection. Some information is needed to keep a player registered, so deleting it may end that player's participation.
  • Account details. Much of your family's information can be updated directly in your PlayMetrics account.
  • Club email. You can unsubscribe from club news using the link in any email. Team and safety messages about a registered player will still be sent.
  • Other jurisdictions. Residents of some states, such as California, may have additional privacy rights.

To make any of these requests, email info@virginiavalorfc.org. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Links to other websites

Our Website links to other sites for your convenience, such as PlayMetrics, league websites and our club stores with soccer.com and ISlide. Those sites are not operated by Virginia Valor FC and have their own privacy policies, which you should review when you visit them. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of sites we do not own.

Data security

Virginia Valor FC takes reasonable measures to protect the information you provide, including limiting access to club staff and volunteers who need it. Unfortunately, the transmission of information over the internet is not completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information sent to our Website. We are not responsible for circumvention of any privacy settings or security measures on our Website.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this Privacy Policy on this page, and the date it was last revised is shown at the bottom. Please check this page from time to time for updates.
