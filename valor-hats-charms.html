How to Order

Hats and charms are not sold online. Send one email with everything you want, and the office will set it aside for you.

Email the office

Write to info@virginiavalorfc.org with your name, phone number and how many hats and charms you want.

Get a reply

The office confirms your order, your total and a time to pick it up.

Pick it up

Collect your order at the Valor office in Chantilly at the time you set.
