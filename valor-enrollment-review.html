Summary

Enrollment is down 17% in two seasons

Valor has 689 travel players registered this season. At the same date in each of the four seasons through 2024-25 it had about 830, and last season 760.

  1. Over six seasons, nearly all of the loss is at U15 and older.U17-U19 went from 201 players to 106, and U15-U16 from 216 to 178. U9-U14 has 405 players, against 420 in 2021-22.
    201 to 106U17-U19 players since 2021-22
  2. The last two seasons are different.Through 2024-25, growth at U9-U14 covered the loss of older players. Since then U9-U14 has fallen from 527 to 405, while U15 and older barely changed (300 to 284).
    527 to 405U9-U14 players since 2024-25
  3. Both returning and new players are down.Returning players went from 528 to 475 in two seasons, and new players from 299 to 214.
    299 to 214new players
  4. A smaller share of players return.Leaving out graduating seniors, 72% returned for 2022-23 and 64% for each of the last two seasons. The drop is mostly boys U13 and older.
    72% to 64%players returning
  5. More players leave without signing up for tryouts.That group grew from 10% of the roster to 15%. The share who sign up and don't return has stayed near 20%.
    10% to 15%left without signing up for tryouts
  6. Where a destination is recorded, it is another club.This includes 11 players who moved to Fairfax Virginia Union (FVU) this season.
    11players to FVU this season

All player counts are as of October 7 of each season, so the seasons compare evenly. Between 11 and 40 more players registered after that date in each earlier season.

Older players

Half the U17-U19 players, half the teams

Valor has about half the U17-U19 players it had in 2021-22, and half the teams for them.

Fewer players stay through the older ages. Of U16 to U18 players who were not graduating, 64% returned for 2022-23 and 51% for this season.

Classes shrink more on the way up. The boys who were U15 in 2021-22 numbered 72, and 34 were here at U18. The boys who were U15 in 2023-24 numbered 48, and 22 are here at U18. For girls the same two classes went from 43 to 31, and from 42 to 23.

New players at these ages held steady, at 28 to 33 a season. Returning players at U17-U19 fell from 140 in 2022-23 to 73.

Senior teams. U18 and U19 teams went from 7 in 2021-22 to 5 in each of the next three seasons and 3 in each of the last two. Since 2024-25 the two ages have shared teams, and this season has no team for players born in 2007-08.

Retention

Boys return far less often from U13 on

Leaving out graduating seniors, 72% of players returned for 2022-23, 68% for each of the next two seasons and 64% for each of the last two. Girls held between 66% and 75%. Boys fell to 56% for 2025-26 and 63% for this season.

In the last two seasons, 42% of U15 boys and 41% of U17 boys came back, down from 58% and 63%. Girls held through U15, then dropped to 55% at U16 and U17.

Tryouts

The rise in leaving is players who don't sign up for tryouts

About one in five players signs up for tryouts and doesn't return every year, and that hasn't changed. Players who leave without signing up grew from 10% of the roster to 15%. Signing up is not the same as attending: in the three seasons with usable check-in records, 18% to 29% of the players who signed up and left have no check-in mark.

Older boys account for most of the rise: 30% of boys U15 and older left without signing up in the last two seasons, up from 18%. The records don't show where players who don't sign up go.

Other clubs. The sheets name another club for 20, 37, 29, 28 and 37 players over the five seasons, including FVU. That number has not risen the way leaving has. Herndon, VSA, Loudoun, McLean, Vienna and BRYC appear most often.

FVU. 11 players moved to FVU this season, against 2 in each of the two seasons before and none earlier. All were on a top team.

Declines with no reason ran between 45 and 61 for four seasons, then fell to 37.

Financial assistance

About 10% of players request aid

Requests ran 53 to 63 a season through 2023-24 and 70 to 79 since. The increase began the year registration moved to PlayMetrics. New families ask most: 13% to 16% of new players, against 6% to 8% of returning players. Since 2024-25, players who requested aid have returned at the same rate as everyone else.
