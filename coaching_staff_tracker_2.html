Import Coaches from a Google Form / Sheet or CSV

There's no direct live connection to Google Forms/Sheets from this file (it runs entirely in your browser, offline). The reliable way in: open your Sheet → File > Download > Comma Separated Values (.csv) → upload it below.

If your Sheet is shared as "Anyone with the link can view", you can instead paste its URL below and try fetching it directly — this sometimes works, but browsers often block it, so the file upload is the dependable option.

Suggested Google Form questions for coaches to fill out: Full Name, Email, Phone, Role, License Track/Type, License Level, Certificate Number, Issue Date, Expiry Date, and a checkbox list "Which upcoming course(s) do you plan to attend?" (using the exact course names from the Upcoming Courses tab).
