Valor Rising

Valor Rising runs alongside your child's rec season. Once a week, Valor staff coaches run a session focused on the ball: dribbling, first touch and basic decision-making. There is no tryout and no team placement, and players work in small groups with children their own age.

  • One session a week with Valor staff coaches, all fall
  • Small groups, so every player gets attention and touches
  • A Valor training jersey to keep, worn across fall, winter and spring sessions
  • No tryout, no team commitment, no pressure to continue

Valor Futures

Valor Futures places U8 players in the U8 Futures League run across the Fairfax Virginia Union, so your child trains with Valor and plays against other local clubs. Games are 5v5 on futsal-size fields, which means more touches, more decisions and more time on the ball. Scores and standings are not kept during the season.

  • 5v5 games, four field players and a keeper, two 25-minute halves
  • Development first: no standings or scores in the regular season
  • 4v4 scrimmage festivals for extra game time
  • Winter programming in development at The Fieldhouse. Spring 2027 mirrors the fall format.
