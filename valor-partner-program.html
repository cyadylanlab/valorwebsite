Two clubs, one pathway

Our local reach

Rooted in Fairfax County

Valor's growth comes from recruitment and steady outreach to new players and families.

Valor reaches about 700 players and families across Northern Virginia, West Virginia and Maryland. Home is Chantilly, Herndon and Centreville, and players also travel in from Loudoun, Arlington and Prince William Counties.

Virginia Valor FCThe pathway
How the club fits together

From recreational to ECNL

A player can grow up inside one pathway, from recreational soccer at U4 to national league play at U19. Fairfax Virginia Union sits at the top, Valor's travel levels run through the middle, and recreational soccer through CYA and SYA is the base.

Alongside the pathway: Competitive Futsal in winter, USL-Y and PRO U23 in summer, and goalkeeper training through the travel season.
