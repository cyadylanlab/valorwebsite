The Program

How it works

Valor Futures places U8 players in a structured league run across the Fairfax Virginia Union, so your child trains and plays alongside and against other local clubs. Games are 5v5 on futsal-dimension fields, which means more touches, more decisions, and more time on the ball than a full-sided game. Scores and standings are not kept during the season. The focus is development, not results.
