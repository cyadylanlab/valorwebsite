    Before you start

    Two quick things: your personal survey code, and how you want your answers recorded.

    Your personal survey code

    Every parent and guardian on a PlayMetrics account receives their own survey link and code by email. Here is what it does:

    • It confirms you are a current Valor family.Only codes the club sent can open the survey, so every response comes from a Valor parent.
    • It counts your players.You can complete one survey for each player linked to your email, and no response gets counted twice.
    • Each parent can take part.If two parents or guardians are on your PlayMetrics account, each of you receives a separate link.
    • No one can answer in your name.Your code only exists in your inbox, so please do not forward it.

    Your code was filled in from your email link.

    Cannot find your survey email?

    Enter the email on your PlayMetrics account and we will send your link again. Check spam and promotions folders too.

    How your answers are recorded

    You decide. We get the most out of responses we can follow up on, so we encourage you to include your email. If you would rather stay anonymous, that is completely fine, and your feedback counts the same.
