Before you go

What to know

Who can come

Open to all players, whether or not they play for Valor. Bring a friend from school, a rec teammate, or a sibling.

What to bring

Cleats, shin guards, a ball, a full water bottle, and weather-appropriate layers. Full day players should pack a lunch and snacks.

If a camp is full

Every camp has a waitlist. Add your player and you will hear from us directly if a spot opens up.

Where it happens

All eight camps are held at EC Lawrence Park. Field assignments go out by email the week of camp.

Weather

Camps run in most conditions. If weather forces a cancellation, families are notified by email and text as early as possible.

Questions

Email dsutherland@virginiavalorfc.org and we will get you sorted before you book.
