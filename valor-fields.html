Leave the field

Lightning and Thunder

Leave the field immediately. Play can resume only after 30 minutes pass with no more lightning or thunder.

Snow and ice

Turf Fields

Closed whenever there is snow or ice.

After rain

Grass Fields

Closures after rain are announced in PlayMetrics by 3 PM. One field or all fields may be closed.
