Virginia Valor FC is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to developing youth soccer players. It is the travel club of Chantilly Youth Association and Southwestern Youth Association, built as one pathway for both communities.

Our approach

Four Stages, One Player

Each age band has its own priorities, so what a U9 player works on is different from what a U17 player works on. The aim at every stage is a better player and a stronger person.

Stage 01U9toU10Begin the Journey

Players discover a passion for the game, learn to work as a team and make friends. The early years focus on fun, engagement and basic skills, along with cooperation, respect and discipline.

  • Fun in learning. Engaging games that introduce skills and keep every player involved.
  • Skills and growth. Fundamentals and life lessons through age-appropriate drills and small-sided games.
  • Teamwork, respect and self-confidence. Team activities, positive discipline and recognition of individual effort.
  • Active, healthy lifestyles. Joyful physical activity and conversations about nutrition.
Stage 02U11toU12Cultivating Champions

Coaching and challenge build resilience and character. Players turn potential into skill, learn to handle failure and success, and develop a growth mindset ahead of their teenage years.

  • Advanced skill development. Technical work under game pressure to sharpen decision-making.
  • Challenge and overcome. Demanding drills and goal-setting that build problem-solving and resilience.
  • Reflection and teamwork. Constructive feedback and collaboration, treating results as lessons.
  • Character and preparation. Hard work, discipline, leadership and exposure to higher-level competition.
Stage 03U13toU15Elevating Excellence

Training intensifies, with high-level technical, tactical and physical work and competitive matches and tournaments that bring exposure to college recruiters.

  • Elite competitive training. High-level drills, conditioning and regular competition.
  • Strategic and tactical excellence. Game analysis and applying complex strategy in real time.
  • Leadership and balance. Roles such as captaincy, and balancing soccer, school and personal life, with attention to mental health.
  • Preparation for the future. College recruitment guidance, life skills and community volunteering.
Stage 04U16toU19Legacy and Beyond

Players mentor younger teammates, prepare for what comes after youth soccer and leave a legacy of leadership at the club.

  • Advanced technical mastery. Elite refinement and high-intensity training for college or professional play.
  • Tactical expertise. Advanced strategy, tactical flexibility and match analysis.
  • Mentorship and leadership. Mentoring programs for younger players and leadership workshops.
  • College, career and life. Recruitment support, networking, and sessions on life skills, financial literacy and career planning.

The benchmarks Valor coaches work toward in each of the four areas of player development.

Stage 01

U9 to U10

Begin the Journey

Technical

  • Fundamentals: dribbling, passing, receiving, shooting
  • Ball mastery with both feet
  • Individual skill through fun games

Tactical

  • Creating space and positional play
  • Basic attacking and defending in small-sided games
  • Decision-making through play

Physical

  • Coordination, balance and agility
  • Soccer as an enjoyable way to exercise
  • General fitness for young athletes

Psychosocial

  • Teamwork and communication
  • Confidence and enjoyment
  • Respect for coaches, teammates and opponents

Tactical

Physical

  • Agility and speed that account for growth spurts

Psychosocial

Benefits of collaboration

Two Clubs, One Pathway

CYA and SYA formed Virginia Valor FC together. Combining the two travel programs gives players more than either club could offer on its own, while each club keeps its own recreational program.

More opportunities

Players from both clubs, recreational to elite, get access to more development and programming options, including the Club Champions League.

Coach and staff development

More resources for coach education and staff development, at a higher level than either club could reach alone.

Benefits of scale

Administrative efficiencies are reinvested in the player experience, and fundraising across CYA, SYA and Valor grows.

Long-term planning

Room for strategic projects such as joint recreational events, tournaments, facilities and wider access to the game.

Better placement

Top players get a more competitive team, older players get exposure to the top level, and newer travel players can compete with less pressure.

Expanded coaching staff

More than 45 staff coaches who coordinate practices, share expertise and grow their knowledge together.

Recreational soccer

CYA and SYA keep separate recreational programs, with more crossover leagues, events and volunteer coach education.

Community outreach

Shared resources for community events, including charity fundraisers, give-back programs and toy donation drives.
