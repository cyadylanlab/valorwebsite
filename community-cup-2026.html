Check in 30 minutes early.

Report to the game-site headquarters before your first match. Bring your roster, proof of age and team check-in form. Keep signed medical releases with your team at every game.

01 Rosters & player eligibility
  • Teams need at least 3 players and no more than 6. Four or five is a typical roster.
  • Enter the division of your oldest player. Tournament organizers may combine divisions.
  • Girls’ divisions are reserved for girls. Girls may also play in boys’ divisions.
  • Players must have hard-copy or digital proof of age at check-in. A birth certificate or other acceptable proof of age must be supplied when requested.
02 Equipment & uniforms
  • Every player must wear shin guards and soccer-appropriate footwear. Indoor venues may have restrictions on cleats.
  • Game balls are provided. U8–U12 divisions use a size 4 ball.
  • Wear matching shirts. Jersey numbers are optional.
  • Bring two contrasting shirt colors so teams can resolve color conflicts.
03 Field, goal box & scoring
  • Fields measure approximately 24–30 yards by 16–20 yards; dimensions may vary with the venue.
  • The goal box measures 8 feet across and 4 feet deep. Players can move through it but cannot play the ball inside it.
  • A defender touching the ball in the box gives the opponent a goal. An attacker touching it in the box gives the defending team a goal kick.
  • A ball that comes to rest in the box results in a goal kick for the defending team.
  • A scoring shot must be taken from the attacking half.
04 Match format & substitutions
  • Play two 12-minute halves with a 2-minute halftime.
  • A match ends as soon as either team scores its 12th goal.
  • One-day events provide at least four games, potentially six or seven with playoffs. Multi-day formats provide at least six, potentially eight or more with playoffs.
  • Substitute during a stoppage with the referee’s permission.
  • Pool matches can finish tied; playoff matches require a winner.
  • A team trailing by six goals may add a fourth player. Remove that player when the deficit falls to five.
05 Restarts & special playing rules
  • No offside. Sliding is prohibited and results in an indirect free kick.
  • Heading is permitted in U11 and older divisions.
  • Defenders stay at least 10 feet from the ball at dead-ball restarts.
  • Restart within five seconds, subject to referee discretion.
  • Use kick-ins in place of throw-ins. Restarts are indirect except for corner kicks and penalties.
  • Take goal kicks from the end line outside the goal box. Kickoffs may travel in any direction.
  • Take penalties from the center spot, with all other players behind midfield. A missed penalty does not remain live.
06 Standings, tiebreakers & playoffs

Pool standings award 3 points for a win, 1 for a tie and 0 for a loss. A forfeit is recorded as a 5–0 loss.

Resolve standings ties in this order:

  1. Head-to-head result, when exactly two teams are tied.
  2. Goal difference.
  3. Fewest goals allowed.
  4. Most goals scored.
  5. A system-generated coin toss.

Tied playoff matches

Play one three-minute golden-goal period. If no one scores, the three players who finished the game take part in a penalty shootout.

07 Conduct & tournament authority
  • Referees can issue cards to players or coaches. A red-carded player leaves the current match and is suspended for the next one.
  • The Tournament Director may remove players, coaches or teams for misconduct. When the responsible coach cannot be identified, the head coach is sanctioned.
  • Referee abuse is not tolerated. Unsporting conduct can lead to removal or disqualification of teams, coaches or spectators.
  • The Tournament Director decides situations these rules do not address.
08 Tournament-day check-in
  • Check in at your game-site headquarters 30 minutes before the first match.
  • Bring an official team roster, either the tournament form or one produced by your club. Jersey numbers are not required.
  • Have a hard copy or digital proof of age for every player.
  • The team coach or parent must certify on the team check-in form that signed medical releases are in their possession for every player. Keep those releases at every game; they are not collected as check-in documents.
  • Tournament, club or other generic medical release forms may be used.
