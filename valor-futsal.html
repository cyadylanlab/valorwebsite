The program runs in two blocks. Each block is three practices with review, where coaches break down what players are working on and correct it on the court, followed by a match against another club. That makes six practices and two matches in all.

Winter development

Training focus

Through our structured two-month futsal program, players can expect to see progress in technical development, decision-making, confidence and tactical understanding.

The greatest benefit goes beyond becoming a better futsal player. It's about learning to transfer these skills directly to the pitch, where players can apply what they've learned in competitive game situations.

Our goal is to develop players who see the game earlier, execute under pressure, support their teammates intelligently and consistently make better decisions on the ball. We are committed to developing these qualities and helping every player bring them to the outdoor game.

Israel AlabiU11/U12 Boys and Girls Director, Virginia Valor FC
