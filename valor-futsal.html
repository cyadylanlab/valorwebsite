Two winter offerings

Two halves of the same floor

Every Valor player can play indoors on Saturdays as part of club dues. Competitive Futsal is a separate, optional program with its own fee and registration.

Saturday Winter Play Series

No additional cost

In-house scrimmages at the CYA Fieldhouse, open to all Valor players.

  • 40-minute scrimmages each week
  • Players mixed within two age groups for balanced, competitive games
  • Keeps players active and comfortable on the ball through the winter

Program focus

What players work on

Winter is a good time to work on what coaches flagged in fall evaluations. The hard court and smaller, heavier ball make every touch and decision count.

  • Ball mastery and comfort in tight spaces Technical
  • Speed of play and first touch Technical
  • 1v1 attacking and defending Duels
  • Tactical understanding and small-group play Tactical
  • Creating and using space Tactical
  • Confidence in possession and decision-making Decisions
  • Shooting, finishing and scoring Finishing
  • Resilience, risk-taking and self-confidence Mentality
