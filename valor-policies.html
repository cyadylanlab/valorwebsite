Player-Parent Commitment

Families also agree to the Travel Commitment letter included in the registration confirmation email. The full commitment form is available as a PDF.

Year-long commitment
  • The commitment covers the full soccer year: fall, winter and spring.
  • Players who leave mid-year receive no refund.
  • Under league rules, a player cannot transfer to another ECNL regional or national club within the same seasonal year, whether the player is in VPSL, NCSL or another league.
  • An ECNL-R rostered player may transfer to another ECNL-R team in the same season only if the family has moved beyond reasonable travel distance and both Directors approve.
  • The club may suspend or cancel some or all of the fall or spring season at its discretion if state or local youth sports guidelines require it.
Financial obligation
  • Parents accept the financial terms in the travel player and parent commitment letter, and the club's enforcement of them.
  • The $400 deposit paid when accepting a team position is non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Families who leave before the end of the soccer year owe all club fees for the full year, paid before a release is processed.
  • Families who leave for any reason still owe team fees, including events held after the release that were budgeted before it was requested.
  • No money paid, donated or fundraised is refunded if a player leaves.

For parents

Parent Code of Conduct

Reported misconduct by club parents results in disciplinary action against players, parents or both.

During matches, stand on the opposite side of the field from the coaches where possible, away from the touchline and the goal box areas.

Disparaging comments toward referees and opposing parents are strongly discouraged.

Respect other teams' staff and match facilities, follow each facility's and host club's rules, and represent the club well when visiting.

Players are handed to the coach at the coach's discretion, normally 45 minutes before kickoff, and prepare from 30 minutes before. After that, keep your distance and trust the coach.

Before games, provide water, enough to drink and a mid-game snack if needed, so players are not distracted during play or at halftime.

During play, encourage and applaud good play from both teams, and support players who make mistakes.

Put player development ahead of results and scorelines. Do not cheer one-sided, high-scoring results.

Do not track or compare playing time. Coaches decide playing time.

For a serious injury, enter the field only after officials stop play and call for a trainer, or when the coach asks for medical or parent help. Coaches handle minor injuries.

Whatever the score, applaud both teams at the end of every match.

For important game-day matters, wait 24 hours, then email or call the coach to arrange a meeting away from the field.

After games, tell your player you enjoy watching them play. Avoid discussing tactics, criticizing play, commenting on officiating or being negative.

Encourage players to watch professional matches to learn and compare ideas they find difficult.

Keep coaches informed about injuries, illness and anything with teammates or opponents that could affect performance.

Club Policies

The policies below apply to players, families, coaches and staff across all Valor programs.

Abuse training

Coaching staff must stay current on the abuse prevention training required by the leagues (VPSL, ECNL RL, NCSL and USL-Y) and governing bodies (VYSA and US Club Soccer). Training is provided by SafeSport, and every coach completes the courses each year.

Anti-bullying and player misconduct

Bullying is not allowed at practices, games or any Valor event. Bullying means insulting, intimidating, humiliating or degrading conduct, in writing, verbally, physically or electronically, whether once or repeatedly. Anyone aware of bullying must report it, and people who report will be supported.

  • Parents report to the team coach or coaching director. If it continues, escalate to the Valor coaching director or executive director. Police may be consulted if necessary.
  • The bullied player's parents are informed immediately and may be asked to meet with the coach or coaching director. Strict confidentiality is kept.
  • The parents of the player responsible are contacted immediately and asked to meet. The behavior is investigated and stopped.
  • A first offense focuses on changing behavior and reconciliation, considering age, maturity and severity. Persistent bullying leads to disciplinary action, which may include suspension from practices or games.
  • The situation is monitored after it is resolved.
Attendance

The head coach and director record attendance at every practice. Players and parents should tell the head coach and director about absences early and clearly.

U7 to U10

Attendance is taken. Players may make up a session with another team after staff confirm it. Playing time may be reduced when practice time is less than teammates'.

U11 to U12

Practices and games are not optional for multi-sport players. Sessions missed for illness, school, church or family must be made up, with staff confirmation first. Playing time may be reduced.

U13 to U18

All practices and games are required, except for illness or family and school events approved by staff in advance. Missed practices are made up with another team, and the player tells staff which session they will attend.

U15 to U18 White, Black and Silver

A second sport is possible with staff approval, at staff discretion. Players are not rewarded for missing practice.

Gold teams

Missing State Cup, ECNL-RL or regional tournament games is never acceptable.

Coaches code of ethics

To players

  • Never put winning above player safety and welfare.
  • Teach play within the Laws of the Game and their spirit, and never teach unsporting behavior.
  • Do not tolerate inappropriate behavior or encourage breaking rules, including Valor bylaws and policies.
  • Do not block players from advancing to higher-level training or competition within Valor.
  • Send injured players to proper medical care and follow professional treatment instructions.

To opponents and the game

  • Show opponents respect and never encourage intentional injury.
  • Avoid physical or verbal confrontation with opposing players, coaches or supporters.
  • Know the Laws of the Game, teach them, and take responsibility for players, officials and spectators connected to the team.

To officials

  • Treat officials with respect and courtesy, and never incite misconduct toward them.
  • Use your influence to stop spectator misconduct toward officials and opponents.
  • Dispute decisions only through methods allowed by the Laws, competition rules or Valor policy.

Recruiting

  • Follow registration and recruiting rules.
  • Do not recruit Valor members to other clubs or solicit the transfer of a Valor team.
  • Do not misrepresent Valor programs to recruit players, including within Valor.
  • Stop recruiting a player when the player or guardian asks, and do not let others recruit on your behalf.
Concussion awareness

Coaching staff stay current on the concussion awareness training and protocols required by the leagues and governing bodies. Training is provided through a third-party certification company, and every Valor coach is trained.

COVID

Valor follows Fairfax County Health Department and CDC procedures and policies.

Electronic communication
  • Coaches and trainers do not communicate directly with players, whatever the player's age or team.
  • Staff may not connect with players on social media except through an approved Valor forum.
  • Coach and team communication goes through the club's team app. When a call or message is needed, it goes to the parent, not the player.
  • Team or player websites using Valor's logos, images or likeness need approval from the Director of Operations before going public.
  • Always represent Valor well online, and avoid disparaging Valor or its competitors. Violations may lead to suspension or dismissal.
Fundraising
  • Team fundraising needs advance approval from the Valor Director of Operations.
  • The team manager first checks for conflicts with the Valor fundraising coordinator, then emails a summary and fund-handling plan to info@virginiavalorfc.org with the subject "Valor Fundraising".
  • All funds go into the Valor team account. Outside accounts are not allowed.
  • Cash and gift cards are submitted in a sealed envelope initialed by two people, labeled with the team name, team manager and coach.
  • The Valor administrator records and reconciles every team account each quarter and shares copies with each team manager and the Director of Operations.
  • Unused funds at season end move to the Valor general fund as a donation.
Guest playing

Within Valor

  • Teams play three to five tournaments a year and at least one league.
  • Guest play is occasional, for team needs and player development, and guests should not take significant playing time from rostered players.
  • The player or parent contacts their director, coaches need Director of Coaching approval, and paperwork is completed in advance.

With another club

  • No Valor player may guest for a team playing against a Valor team.
  • Requests are made at least one week before the event, and the player's Valor team must not have a game or tournament that weekend.
  • The other team's coach contacts the Director of Coaching for permission. Player cards are released only after both steps, for 72 hours.
Harassment

Valor prohibits sexual, racial and ethnic harassment of any employee by co-workers, managers, guests, members or vendors. This includes sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexual or racial jokes, ethnic slurs and harassing language or conduct. Violations can be grounds for immediate termination.

Non-solicitation

Solicitation or distribution at any place Valor teams train, play or meet, or through Valor's electronic channels, needs written approval from the Board of Directors. Merchandise sales are limited to Valor's uniform and spirit wear partners and Valor-sponsored events, and Valor or tournament names and logos may not be used for personal solicitation.

Staff background checks

Every staff member completes the background check required by Valor, the leagues and governing bodies before they can be employed or coach.

Tournament hotels

Host clubs set hotel rules, such as approved hotels and two-night minimums, and Valor follows them. Tournament acceptance depends on following host hotel rules, and teams that book outside the process may lose their place. The team manager books rooms within host policies, aiming to house all families in one hotel.

Weather

Outdoor sessions are canceled by temperature and heat index thresholds, players leave the field at the first lightning or thunder for at least 30 minutes, turf is closed in snow or ice, and grass closures are posted in PlayMetrics by 3 PM. Full weather policy

Refunds
  • No refunds once a player commits, including the non-refundable $400 commit fee. Fees remain the family's responsibility through the end of the season year, even if the player leaves or is dismissed.
  • Exceptions: season-ending injuries (not temporary ones) and a family move due to military orders.
  • VYSA-carded players must pay all dues before release to another club. US Club Soccer players cannot join another US Club-affiliated team until the end of the seasonal year.
  • USL-Y summer: 50% refund before April 1 of the competition year, and no refunds after May 1.
