TravelSoccer

Virginia Valor FC is the travel club of CYA and SYA. Travel soccer is a 10-month program, August through June, for U9 to U19, coached by professionally licensed staff. Valor players have gone on to state, regional, national, college and professional levels.

The Pathway

Players can enter at any stage. Most start in CYA or SYA recreational soccer, try a pre-travel program at U7 or U8, and move into travel soccer from U9.

Development curriculum

What Each Age Focuses On

Valor's curriculum sets technical, tactical, physical and psychosocial benchmarks for each stage. The full benchmarks are on the Why Valor page.

U9toU10

Begin the Journey

Fun first. Dribbling, passing, receiving and shooting through small-sided games, with teamwork, respect and self-confidence built alongside.

U11toU12

Cultivating Champions

Advanced technique, position-specific skills and tactical awareness. Players learn to handle success and failure and build resilience.

U13toU15

Elevating Excellence

High-level training, competitive matches and tournaments with college recruiter exposure, leadership roles and balancing soccer with school.

U16toU19

Legacy and Beyond

Elite technical refinement, game intelligence, mentoring younger players and preparing for college or professional play.

What families sign up for

The Commitment

Making a travel team is a commitment of time and money for the full season year.

Every practice, game and tournament

Players

Attend every practice, game and tournament in the fall primary season. Conflicts with other sports or social plans are resolved in favor of travel commitments. Players are encouraged to play other sports in winter and spring and should honor those commitments too.

Support and attendance

Parents

Make participation possible and come to games and tournaments. Each team sets its own off-season expectations, so talk through any conflicts with your coach.
