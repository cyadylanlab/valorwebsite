Tryouts and evaluations

Common Questions

Anything not covered here goes to your program's Director of Coaching.

Is it too late to join for 2026-27?
Not necessarily. Formal tryouts have finished, but Valor evaluates players for open roster spots on a rolling basis. Email your program's Director of Coaching to ask about your age group.
Do I need to register for an evaluation?
No formal registration is needed to attend an evaluation. The Director of Coaching will set up a time with you.
Which age group does my player try out for?
The age group for their date of birth under the August 1 to July 31 cutoff. Tryouts and team placement follow the new age groups.
Can my player try out for an older age group?
Playing up is a technical and coaching decision based on skill, soccer maturity and development needs. It is the exception, and technical staff review each case.
What does it cost to join?
$2,850 for the 2026-27 season year, plus a $400 non-refundable commit deposit due when you accept a team invitation ($3,250 in total). Uniforms are bought separately.
Is financial help available?
Yes. Financial aid toward club dues is available for qualified families and is applied for during registration in PlayMetrics.

Younger players, no tryout needed

U7 and U8 Players

Travel teams start at U9. Players in the U7 and U8 age groups start with Valor's pre-travel programs instead of a tryout.

U7 and U8 · Open enrollment

Valor Rising

One coached session a week with Valor staff coaches, alongside the rec season in the fall and indoors at the CYA Fieldhouse in the winter. No tryout, no team commitment.

$200per winter session
