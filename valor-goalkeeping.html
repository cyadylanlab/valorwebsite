Meet our Director of Goalkeeping

Drew Leonard

Drew played youth club soccer in Southern California with no formal goalkeeper training until high school. The summer before his senior year, he attended Dr. Joe Machnick's #1 Keeper's Camp, which changed his path. He wants Valor keepers to get back what they put into the program.

Email Drew Leonard

UCLA and semi-pro

Playing

Goalkeeper at UCLA, 1983 to 1987. Semi-pro Men's Open soccer with Los Angeles area teams, 1988 to 1994.

UCLA and UC Berkeley

Coaching

Founded a goalkeeping school in the Pasadena area in 1989. Returned to UCLA in 1990 as goalkeeper coach for the men's and later the women's program, and trained US keepers who went on to professional and international play. Later spent two years as goalkeeper coach at UC Berkeley.

Leesburg, Virginia

Beyond soccer

A career-changing injury kept him from coaching and playing until 2002. He spent about 20 years in the data center industry in marketing and operations, and lives in Leesburg, Virginia.
